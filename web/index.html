    3 Why approximation and revaluation differ

    Local approximation = Delta × spot change + ½ Gamma × spot change² + Vega × volatility-point change + Theta × calendar days. Higher-order and cross terms are omitted. Use full revaluation for large shocks.

    Position details

    Methods and scope

    European options use Black–Scholes–Merton with continuous dividend yield; American options use a 300-step CRR tree. Both use the same Python engine for a single underlying and currency, with long and short positions.

    Approximate P&L includes Delta, Gamma, Vega and Theta. European Delta/Gamma/Vega are analytic before expiry with positive volatility; American Delta/Gamma use tree-node differences. Other sensitivities use finite differences. Large shocks, exercise boundaries and finite tree resolution can cause error; prioritize full revaluation.

    This is static scenario analysis, not a trading backtest, volatility forecast or investment recommendation. It excludes volatility-surface calibration, discrete dividends, actual settlement and fees. All examples are invented; separate academic QQQ research and historical return figures are not incorporated.

    Up to 10 positions and 10 custom scenarios per run; the separate price-range task supports up to 30 sampled nodes. Browser calculations can be cancelled.
