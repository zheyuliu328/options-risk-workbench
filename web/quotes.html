Methods and limits

European contracts use Black–Scholes–Merton; American contracts invert the fixed-step Cox–Ross–Rubinstein tree, without a European fallback. Rates and dividend yields are continuous, time is ACT/365, and volatility is flat per contract. Discrete cash dividends and fitted volatility surfaces are unsupported.

Boundary prices, weak price sensitivity, invalid quotes and search limits remain visible. The 500% volatility cap is a numerical search limit, not a market validity rule. Declared dates do not authenticate synchronized market observations.

Inputs are cleared on refresh. Explicit transfer temporarily uses this tab’s session storage and is consumed by the portfolio page. Download the quote report first if you need to retain its provenance.
